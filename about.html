// About

The training framework that learns from you.

Lambda is a resistance training framework built around your data. The principles that drive progress are universal — Lambda helps you apply them to your training, your body, and your goals.

// Features

Stop assuming.
Start knowing.

Lambda turns your training into a clear, evolving picture of what's working, what isn't, and what comes next. Not someone else's data. Yours.

A framework, not a program

Powerlifting, bodybuilding, calisthenics — Lambda works across paradigms because it's built on principles, not prescriptions. The method stays yours.

Your evidence

Lambda builds from your own training history. Because your data is the most reliable evidence there is.

Reflection as a training variable

Mood, perception, notes. Lambda captures the subjective side of training and puts it to work alongside your numbers.

Diagnose your plateaus

Real oversight over your training history. When progress stalls, see exactly where — and why.
