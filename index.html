// How it works

A loop, not a workout plan.

Resistance training is a feedback system. Lambda closes the loop between what you do, what you measure, and what comes next.

01 / Log

Capture every set

Load, reps, RIR, and tempo logged in seconds. Lambda fills in the rest from your history.

02 / Analyze

Read the signal

Volume, intensity, and fatigue are continuously modeled across muscle groups and movement patterns.

03 / Adapt

Get the next session

Lambda prescribes the next workout — progressing, holding, or deloading where the data calls for it.
