// Features

Stop assuming.
Start knowing.

Log sets, build programs, track PRs — then go further. Lambda connects your training data to the questions that actually matter about your progress, your consistency, and your readiness.

Plan

Set your goals, build programs, and map out your training sessions. Define your intended frequency and level of commitment — the baseline Lambda measures everything else against.

Capture

Log sets, reps, and weight for every workout. Record non-training variables — sleep, nutrition, hydration, bodyweight — that give your session data context. Fast, opinionated entry with no unnecessary fields.

Analyze

See how what you’ve captured compares to what you planned. Lambda helps you understand why your results look the way they do — identifying where the gap between expectation and outcome comes from.

No bloatware.

Clean session diaries. Clear programme views. Fast PR lookup and coaching tools. Every screen has a purpose, every field has a reason. If it isn’t useful, it isn’t there.

// How it works

A framework for
decision making.

You can’t steer what you don’t see. The framework needs signal from real training — that’s what the Lambda app is for: capture, organise, surface. Then you decide. Principles inform the move; the app shows whether it landed.

01 / Design

Build your plan

Start with a plan built around your goals, schedule, and method. Lambda gives you the structure to be intentional from day one.

02 / Track

Run the plan long enough to learn from it

Log your sets, sessions, and how you felt. Consistency is what makes the data worth reading.

03 / Analyse

Understand what’s actually working

Lambda brings your numbers and your context together — so you can see where your method is, and isn’t, delivering results.

04 / Evolve

Make the call with confidence

You decide what changes — load, frequency, exercise selection, structure. Lambda doesn’t prescribe. It informs. The best decision is always yours to make.
